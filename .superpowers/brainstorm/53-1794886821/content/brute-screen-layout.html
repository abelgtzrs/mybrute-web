The Brute screen: how should it be laid out?

The brute is the home screen of the mobile Lab. All three versions show the same information and the same four bottom tabs (Brute, Teams, Fight, Bench). They differ in how you move through a brute's details and how you switch between brutes. Click the one that feels right.

A. One long page

Everything about the brute in one scroll: identity, stats, kit, inventory, then level-ups. You switch brutes on the Teams tab.
